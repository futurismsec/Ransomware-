## Overview

Ransomware is a growing threat to business continuity. Futurism's AI-driven Ransomware Readiness Assessment evaluates your defenses ahead of an attack, identifies vulnerabilities, and validates your recovery plans. It is pre-incident preparation aimed at keeping your business running if ransomware strikes.

The assessment is built to answer one question: **"Are we truly ready to survive a ransomware attack?"**

## Why It Matters

Ransomware attacks are increasing in volume, sophistication and impact. Even organizations with an established cybersecurity program can be unprepared when an attack happens. This assessment tests that readiness before it is needed.

## What's Included

### 1. Pre-Attack Preparedness Evaluation
- Phishing and social engineering susceptibility
- Remote access hygiene and MFA implementation
- Endpoint protection effectiveness
- Backup architecture review (integrity, immutability, frequency)

### 2. Simulated Ransomware Scenarios
- Controlled simulations that replicate real-world attack paths without harming your environment
- Testing of containment mechanisms such as EDR, AV, XDR and isolation protocols

### 3. Response Plan Analysis
- Review of ransomware-specific Incident Response playbooks
- Alignment with NIST, MITRE and CIS Benchmarks
- Gap identification in containment, communication and escalation workflows

### 4. Recovery Plan Validation
- Testing of backup restoration objectives (RTO/RPO)
- Identification of recovery vulnerabilities, such as encrypted or infected backups
- Validation of data segregation and offline/immutable backups

### 5. Executive Summary and Roadmap
- Ransomware readiness score, graded across security controls, recovery ability and response plans
- Clear, prioritized improvement recommendations
- Business impact and risk exposure analysis
